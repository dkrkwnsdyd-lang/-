# 3-Scene Flow 샘플 (텀블러, 레퍼런스 없음, 특징 영어 번역본 사용)

영어 프롬프트는 Google Flow 에 그대로 붙여 넣는 용도이고, 상품 참고 사진을 함께 첨부하세요.

## Scene 1 — Scroll Stopper / Strong Hook (3s)

**Google Flow Video Prompt**

```
Scene 1 of 3 — Scroll Stopper / Strong Hook. Vertical 9:16 smartphone UGC footage, TikTok commercial energy but real-life look, lively pace with one quick dynamic reframe within the shot, duration 3 seconds, single continuous shot. Who/where: a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible, at a tidy home kitchen counter. Action: The person starts mid-annoyance: the person shows the everyday small frustration this product is meant for (acted out with hands and a mildly annoyed expression), then the product is pulled into frame in the last second. Expression: curious, slightly surprised. Product: Product enters or is partly visible; it is held in the right hand and not yet fully demonstrated. Product Lock — identical to the attached reference photo (shape, proportions, color, logo, printed text, button positions). Camera: close-up, eye-level angle, fast handheld punch-in. Framing keeps the product readable, about 40% of the frame visible. Lighting: soft natural daylight, slightly imperfect real-life exposure. Background: a tidy home kitchen counter, lived-in, not a studio set. Continuity for all 3 scenes: the same ordinary person (a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible), same casual outfit and hairstyle, same location (a tidy home kitchen counter), same soft natural daylight, same single product, same vertical phone framing. Starts: Black-to-live cut; person already mid-action, no intro. Ends: Product held in the right hand, about 40% of it visible, person looking at it. Avoid: extra or deformed fingers; hand or fingers passing through the product; duplicated or multiple products; product changing shape, color or logo between frames; invented buttons, ports or parts; on-screen text, subtitles, captions or watermarks; brand logos other than those printed on the real product; celebrity or lookalike faces; studio commercial lighting or glossy ad look; distorted or unreadable product text; spoken dialogue in the audio.
```

- Voice Over: 텀블러 뚜껑 여는 게 번거로워요, 은근 불편하죠
- Caption: 텀블러 뚜껑 여는 게
번거로워요 불편
- SFX: pop
- CTA 여부: 아니오


## Scene 2 — Product Demo / Hero Use (5s)

**Google Flow Video Prompt**

```
Scene 2 of 3 — Product Demo / Hero Use. Vertical 9:16 smartphone UGC footage, TikTok commercial energy but real-life look, stable and clear, duration 5 seconds, single continuous shot. Who/where: a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible, at a tidy home kitchen counter. Action: The same person holds the product in the right hand and uses the left hand to demonstrate the main stated feature (opens with a one-touch lid), in a single continuous natural motion. Expression: focused, interested. Product: Demonstrates the main stated feature (opens with a one-touch lid); the product stays unchanged, nothing else is added to it. Product Lock — identical to the attached reference photo (shape, proportions, color, logo, printed text, button positions). Camera: close-up, eye-level angle, slightly above the hands, slow push-in. Framing keeps the product readable, about 70% of the frame visible. Lighting: same soft natural daylight as scene 1. Background: the same location as scene 1 (a tidy home kitchen counter). Continuity for all 3 scenes: the same ordinary person (a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible), same casual outfit and hairstyle, same location (a tidy home kitchen counter), same soft natural daylight, same single product, same vertical phone framing. Starts: Matches the end of scene 1: product in the right hand, same framing, now fully in view. Ends: Feature clearly shown, product centered and fully visible for a beat. Avoid: extra or deformed fingers; hand or fingers passing through the product; duplicated or multiple products; product changing shape, color or logo between frames; invented buttons, ports or parts; on-screen text, subtitles, captions or watermarks; brand logos other than those printed on the real product; celebrity or lookalike faces; studio commercial lighting or glossy ad look; distorted or unreadable product text; spoken dialogue in the audio.
```

## Scene 3 — Result / Hero Shot / CTA Ending (5s)

**Google Flow Video Prompt**

```
Scene 3 of 3 — Result / Hero Shot / CTA Ending. Vertical 9:16 smartphone UGC footage, TikTok commercial energy but real-life look, confident closing beat, duration 5 seconds, single continuous shot. Who/where: a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible, at a tidy home kitchen counter. Action: The same person finishes using the product with a relaxed, satisfied expression, then holds it in the right hand toward the camera with a friendly final look and a small nod. Product: Product shown fully and steadily in the right hand as the hero shot; no extra items. Product Lock — identical to the attached reference photo (shape, proportions, color, logo, printed text, button positions). Camera: medium close-up, product occupying about half of the frame, eye-level angle, slow smooth pull-back settling into a steady hero framing. Framing keeps the product readable, about 50% of the frame visible. Lighting: same soft natural daylight as scene 1. Background: the same location as scene 1 (a tidy home kitchen counter). Continuity for all 3 scenes: the same ordinary person (a person filming themselves with a smartphone, face partly visible), same casual outfit and hairstyle, same location (a tidy home kitchen counter), same soft natural daylight, same single product, same vertical phone framing. Starts: Matches the end of scene 2: same position, product visible. Ends: Steady hero frame held for the last second (end-card friendly), no text. Avoid: extra or deformed fingers; hand or fingers passing through the product; duplicated or multiple products; product changing shape, color or logo between frames; invented buttons, ports or parts; on-screen text, subtitles, captions or watermarks; brand logos other than those printed on the real product; celebrity or lookalike faces; studio commercial lighting or glossy ad look; distorted or unreadable product text; spoken dialogue in the audio.
```
